# Orbit Workspace — User Guide

## 1. Getting Started & Authentication
1. Sign in instantly with **Continue with Google**, or switch to **Create Account** to register with your name, email, and a password meeting all 5 strength requirements (8+ characters, uppercase, lowercase, number, special character).
2. Email/password accounts require clicking the email verification link before accessing workspace data.

## 2. Dashboard
- View live metrics across all projects you own or belong to: Total Projects, Total Tasks, Tasks To Do, In Progress, Completed, and Overdue Tasks.
- Update the status of tasks assigned to you directly from the **Assigned to Me** list, or click any task to inspect full details.

## 3. Managing Projects & Teams
- Click **New Project** in the sidebar or Projects page to create a project with start/end dates and status.
- As **Project Owner**, open a project's **Team** tab and enter a registered user's email address to add them as a **Project Member**.
- Owners can edit project details, export Markdown project reports, manage team members, and delete projects.

## 4. Kanban Board, Task List & Calendar
- **Board**: Drag and drop task cards between **To Do**, **In Progress**, and **Completed**, or use the status selector on each card.
- **Task List**: Filter tasks by search query, status, priority, assigned member, or due date (Overdue, Due Soon), and sort by newest, oldest, or due date.
- **Calendar**: View task deadlines plotted across a monthly calendar grid.

## 5. Collaboration & Notifications
- Click any task to open the detail drawer to add link attachments, post comments, or review task history.
- Use the **Discussions** tab for project-wide team updates.
- Open the **Notification Center** (bell icon or sidebar) to review and clear assignment, status, and comment alerts.
